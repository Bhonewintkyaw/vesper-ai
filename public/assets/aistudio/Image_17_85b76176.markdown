## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an ambient, intelligent, and focused cognitive workspace. Designed for builders, researchers, and technical professionals operating in high-concentration environments, the visual character strikes a balance between midnight serenity and cybernetic precision.

The style fuses **Dark Glassmorphism** with **Atmospheric Minimalism**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep obsidian backdrops that recede, allowing luminous violet and electric cyan accents to indicate AI activity, state transitions, and computational intelligence.
- **Glass & Micro-Borders:** Tactile, semi-translucent frosted surfaces anchored by crisp, hairline boundary strokes that retain structural definition in zero-light contexts.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Elimination of loud distractions in favor of soft glow dissipation, refined typographic rhythm, and clear contextual focus.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a cold, deep obsidian scale contrasted by intelligent neon illumination:

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base environment is anchored at `#090A0F` (True Obsidian). Surface container elevations scale through `#0F111A` (Base Plate) and `#181B26` (Floating Surface).
- **Primary Accent (`#8B5CF6` - Neon Violet):** Encapsulates synthesis, AI generation, and top-tier interactive triggers. Used in focused input nodes, primary action pill buttons, and radial ambient halos.
- **Secondary Accent (`#06B6D4` - Electric Cyan):** Signifies telemetry, active streaming, tool execution, and contextual state indicators.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Built on `#1E2333` at default rests, elevating to rgba-tinted cyan/violet rings on active focus states.
- **Text & Contrast Tiers:** Pure White (`#F8FAFC`) for display and key headers, Slate Silver (`#94A3B8`) for body prose and conversation output, and Muted Indigo (`#475569`) for subtle metadata and inactive labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a clean separation between human thought, structured AI responses, and synthetic machine data:

- **Headlines & Titles (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric, modern, and open apertures that remain crisp over dark gradients without feeling harsh.
- **Continuous Reading (Inter):** Highly neutral and legible body copy optimized for extended reasoning streams, prompt inputs, and complex dialogue trees.
- **Machine Interface & Code (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for agent execution traces, token counters, parameter adjustments, syntax-highlighted code blocks, and micro tags.
- Line heights maintain breathing room (`1.5` to `1.6x` on body copy) to prevent dense text blocks from causing visual fatigue against high-contrast dark backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a mobile-first fluid structure anchored to a dynamic 4-column system on mobile hand devices (scaling to 8 columns on foldables and tablets):

- **Horizontal Margins:** Fixed `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins ensure comfortable thumb touch anchors and avoid edge clipping on curved bezel screens.
- **Vertical Chat/Canvas Rhythm:** A persistent vertical flow relying on `space-md` between conversational bubbles, `space-lg` to separate conversational turns, and `space-xl` for sectional transitions.
- **Safe Area Adaptations:** The bottom docking zone reserves an expanded navigation buffer (`calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 1rem)`) to float the primary multimodal prompt input seamlessly above OS controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional top-down drop shadows. Depth is communicated strictly through surface transience, glass refraction, and ambient backlights:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#090A0F`. Non-interactive foundation layer.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Conversation Traces):** Solid `#0F111A` with a 1px border of `#1E2333`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Glass Cards & Prompts):** Translucent background `#181B26` with `80%` opacity, paired with a `backdrop-blur(16px)` and a subtle interior highlight (a top 1px border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and remaining sides in `#1E2333`).
- **Level 3 (Modal Overlays & Popovers):** Translucent background `#181B26` with `92%` opacity, `backdrop-blur(24px)`, and an ambient outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 32px -8px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.
- **AI Streaming Aura:** Active generating blocks display a perimeter or edge gradient breathing between `#8B5CF6` and `#06B6D4` with a diffuse `8px` blur.

## Shapes

The design system implements a curated `Rounded` geometric profile (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px), giving surfaces a modern, ergonomic palm feel without looking overly bulbous.
- **Input Fields & Micro-Pills:** Fully rounded pill curves (`9999px`) are strictly reserved for conversational chips, tool selectors, and send buttons to signify touch points and direct interactive targets.
- **Code Panes & Structural Panels:** Retain tighter `0.5rem` (8px) corners to preserve maximum viewport utility for structured content.

## Components

### Action & Dispatch Buttons
- **Primary Pill (Generate / Send):** Background utilizes a horizontal gradient from `#8B5CF6` to `#7C3AED` with pure white text, an inner top border of `rgba(255,255,255,0.25)`, and a subtle resting glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`). Active/pressed states compress scale slightly (`scale: 0.97`).
- **Secondary Ghost:** Translucent background `rgba(24, 27, 38, 0.6)` with a `1px` stroke in `#1E2333`. Text in `#94A3B8`, transitioning to `#F8FAFC` on press.

### Prompt Input Bar
- Floating docking unit with `backdrop-blur(20px)` over `rgba(15, 17, 26, 0.85)`. 
- Outlined with `#1E2333`, transforming into an electric gradient ring (`#8B5CF6` fading into `#06B6D4`) when focused.
- Accompanied by integrated trailing action icons (voice input, attachment, token limits) in muted slate.

### Conversational Chips & Tool Badges
- Built with `label-mono` typography.
- Inactive badges sit on `rgba(24, 27, 38, 0.8)` with a `#1E2333` border.
- Active or running AI tools display a pulsing cyan dot (`#06B6D4`) alongside an electric cyan hairline outline.

### Message Cells & Reasoning Panels
- **User Message:** Snug right-aligned bubble in `#181B26` with `rounded-2xl` corners (bottom-right tightened), framed with a low-opacity violet border.
- **Agent Intelligence Stream:** Left-aligned full-width canvas container. Transparent background to blend into the canvas, marked by an authoritative, slim vertical glowing cyan/violet lead stroke during streaming.
- **Collapsible Reasoning/Thought Node:** Enclosed in `#0F111A` with a dotted left border, featuring a monospaced accordion header displaying execution time (e.g., `thought for 1.4s`).

### Code Snippets & Syntax Blocks
- Encapsulated within `#0B0D14` containers with rounded 8px corners.
- Header strip containing filename, language indicator, and a one-touch "Copy" icon button in `label-mono`.
- Syntax highlighting centered around electric cyan keywords, violet parameters, and muted grey strings.

### Form Inputs & Toggles
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp-cornered circles and rounded squares with a `#1E2333` base that fill with `#8B5CF6` and a crisp white tick mark upon selection.
- **Sliders & Step Values:** Thin `#1E2333` track with a glowing `#8B5CF6` filled segment and an illuminated `#06B6D4` thumb handle.
